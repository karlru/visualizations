<Chapter id="the-colony" title="The colony" scene="colony-overview" eyebrow="Chapter 01">

The colony has no leader and no map. Each ant sees only the city it stands in and the trails
left by the ants before it.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="the-problem" title="The problem" scene="tour-problem" eyebrow="Chapter 02">

The task is to visit every city once and return to the start. A short tour is hard to find
because the number of possible orderings grows very quickly.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="one-ants-choice" title="One ant's choice" scene="focal-choice" eyebrow="Chapter 03">

Watch one ant at a focal city. Its next move depends on two local signals: how much pheromone
an edge carries and how far away its other end is.

</Chapter>

<Chapter
  id="the-probability-rule"
  title="The probability rule"
  scene="probability-rule"
  eyebrow="Chapter 04"
>

An ant does not pick the shortest edge outright. It samples from a probability distribution
shaped by pheromone and distance, so every allowed city keeps a chance.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="leaving-a-trace" title="Leaving a trace" scene="deposit" eyebrow="Chapter 05">

When an ant completes a tour, it adds pheromone to the edges it used. Shorter tours leave a
stronger trace than longer ones.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="forgetting" title="Forgetting" scene="evaporation" eyebrow="Chapter 06">

Pheromone does not accumulate forever. Evaporation removes a fraction of every trail each
iteration, so stale choices lose their advantage.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="emergence" title="Emergence" scene="emergence" eyebrow="Chapter 07">

No ant plans the whole route, yet the colony's best tour improves. The result emerges from
repeated local choices and a shared environment.

</Chapter>

<Chapter
  id="tuning-the-colony"
  title="Tuning the colony"
  scene="tuning"
  eyebrow="Chapter 08"
>

The weights α and β change how strongly the colony trusts pheromone or distance, while ρ sets
how quickly old trails fade.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="when-it-fails" title="When it fails" scene="limits" eyebrow="Chapter 09">

Ant System is a heuristic, not a guarantee. It can settle on a mediocre tour, and its cost
grows with the size of the problem.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="the-lab" title="The lab" scene="lab" eyebrow="Chapter 10">

The final chapter is a place to compare how the colony behaves under different conditions. The
same local rules can produce a very different search.

</Chapter>
